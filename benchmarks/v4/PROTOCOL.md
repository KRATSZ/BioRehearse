# v4 extension: capability and economic sensitivity

This is a retrospective analysis of the existing 60 workload designs, not a new untouched test set. The v2 specification, scorer and raw results remain unchanged. EPI uses the square root of per-sample elapsed hours times per-sample nominal CNY; the sample denominator excludes controls and technical replication. Mixed-assay sample counts do not establish equivalent biological output. All 1,080 outcomes, including 387 nonreturns, are retained. Paired comparisons require all three repeats valid for both methods; repeated values are averaged within workload, and the workload is the bootstrap unit (10,000 draws, seed 20261019).

Capacity sensitivity recompiles all 60 reference intents under baseline, an additional identical shaker, and an additional identical reader. A clone adds an independent resource at a 0.6 m anchor offset; capability, rate, labware and capacity are copied, and existing inventory, bookings, operator, windows and deadlines remain fixed. Each fresh run has the same 2 s construction/search cap. The runner saves its source and input hashes before solving; all 180 outcomes and plans are retained. Counts and paired changes are descriptive single executions, with no statistical population inference. All returned plans require independent schedule, mass and material-location checks. Geometry, added-site feasibility, calibration, heating transitions, tariffs, depreciation and procurement ROI are outside this scalar-resource sensitivity.

Economic counterfactuals are conditional recovery/repetition scenarios. Neither mutation frequency nor a missing water movement estimates natural error prevalence or biological failure probability. Real tariffs, invoices, recovery outcomes and human planning records must be supplied before realized benefits can be estimated.

Practical comparison protocols are in human-and-vendor-study.md. No human, vendor-matched or physical benchmark result is inferred from source-code review, old projects or unrelated official analysis examples.
