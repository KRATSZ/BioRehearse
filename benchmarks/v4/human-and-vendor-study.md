# Prospective practical comparisons (prepared; no observations collected)

The existing computational suite cannot establish superiority over engineers, vendor software, or physical workflows. These studies are separate additions, not inferred scores in Table 1.

## Manual scheduling
Recruit 3–5 platform engineers. Supply the same station capabilities, inventories, releases, windows, downtime bookings and objective as the algorithms, with a scheduling spreadsheet and complete method graphs. Use 12 workload designs balanced between three and six experiments. Keep the expected schedule/defect labels hidden. Counterbalance workload/order and rehearsal availability across engineers; use disjoint matched cases for conditions to avoid learning an answer by repetition. Record active preparation time, returned makespan, independent violations and correction time, including abandoned attempts. Report every engineer and case, use crossed engineer/workload units, and describe this small study as a pilot; do not treat multiple rows as independent people. Hardware execution is not required for this planning comparison.

## Vendor analysis on a common representable subset
Map each selected liquid-handling stage to a native official device protocol with the same liquid volumes, initial stocks and labware constraints, recording compiler coverage before evaluation. Version and hash the translation, vendor engine and protocol. Run unchanged controls and the same representable mutations with the official analyser; independently label whether each mutation threatens that device operation. Report coverage, conditional detection/false alarms, analysis latency and reasons for unrepresentable cross-station state. An unsupported cross-station error is NA, never a missed detection. Do not compare Opentrons hardware substitutions as if they were the original Fluent configuration. A matched Fluent comparison needs the appropriate Fluent simulator, licensed method and deck/carrier configuration. The available official Opentrons examples do not already supply this matched benchmark.

## Physical timing/assay calibration
Proposed minimum: three independently prepared and executed BCA runs on separate occasions, retaining manual stock preparation/plate transfer. Confirm the actual Fluent deck, iD5 method, labware, SOP and authorized operators before execution. Record station release/start/end, every handoff, loaded stocks, plate IDs, reader output and failures. Compare the preregistered predicted schedule against actual makespan and individual handoff times; report absolute and relative errors for every run. Technical wells do not replace independent runs. Establish quantitative assay acceptance using the facility's validated calibration/QC procedure. Proposed engineering timing targets must be agreed prospectively by platform users; no threshold is inferred from this computational paper. No hardware control is dispatched by the current script.

## External requests and tariffs
Collect 10–20 verbatim requests from platform users before author editing. Two independent laboratory experts label supported intent or needed clarification and reconcile disagreements before model execution. Preserve all wording and outputs; no request-dependent prompt repair. Record author/source independence and method coverage. Before paid model evaluation, verify route access, prices, remaining allowance and total cap; current OpenRouter route returns HTTP401, so no new mainstream-model scores exist.

Obtain the dated facility tariff with internal/external categories, minimum billing units, operator inclusion and consumable inclusion. Collect invoices or actual net reagent prices. Avoid counting included labor/consumables twice. For errors, record prevalence and whether late manual recovery succeeds before assuming an assay repeat. These inputs are needed for expected avoided cost or payback; model rates and mutations cannot substitute for them.
