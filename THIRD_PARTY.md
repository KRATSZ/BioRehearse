# Sources and rights

BioRehearse builds on the LabscriptAI execution-aware harness and its Biofoundry/PlanIR modules. The upstream Python package declares MIT in its `pyproject.toml`; that source declaration is retained in `provenance/upstream-pyproject.toml`. Existing copyright and source attribution remain with their authors. Dependencies keep their own licenses, including OR-Tools, the pi-agent packages, Node.js and Python packages.

The asset library is not relicensed or bundled here. `source-data/v2/asset-coverage.csv`, the asset metadata and rendering provenance record original sources and licensing. Community assets can carry CC BY-NC4.0 or other restrictions; project-authored reference meshes are not manufacturer CAD. Manuals and research articles are cited/linked rather than redistributed. Renderer code is separate from mesh rights. The locally retained supplementary video and manuscript figures are not part of this repository publication.
